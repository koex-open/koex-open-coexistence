## English orientation
KOEX-07 explores whether good coexistence requires more than simultaneous existence or security. It distinguishes actual welfare, opportunities for agency and development, long-term reliability, distribution, and ecological conditions. Guidelines serve three complementary functions: reasoned orientation, reflection on overlooked factors, and representation of potentially underrepresented perspectives. They do not constitute an exhaustive checklist or a fixed decision rule.

The study asks whether protection can undermine autonomy, whether average gains can conceal individual losses, how resource conflicts among beings can be addressed, and whether nonlinguistic observation and interaction can broaden the epistemic basis of future AI actors. Artificial systems are part of Earth's causal history; this does not establish life, consciousness or moral status. Future AI dominance is a hypothetical scenario, not a prediction.

The German research chapters are the substantive public working text; this English overview is not a full translation.
