# Contributing to KOEX / Beiträge zu KOEX

Thanks for helping to improve KOEX. Contributions are welcome **even if you reject some or all current guidelines**. We want better arguments, clearer evidence, and awareness of unconsidered parties — not a consensus performance.

## Suggested channels

- **Discussions:** broad debate, philosophical alternatives, new perspectives; if Discussions are not yet enabled, use a question Issue.
- **Issues:** specific research questions, missing evidence, contradictions, problematic wording, counterexamples, accessibility issues.
- **Pull requests:** concrete improvements to documents, with reasoning and relevant context updates.

## For guideline changes

Please identify: (1) affected `L-xx` code, (2) proposed wording, (3) motivation and assumptions, (4) best counterexample or strongest objection, (5) effects on other guidelines and affected beings, (6) evidence tier: empirical / philosophical / hypothetical / normative, (7) remaining uncertainty, (8) context-document changes. You may report uncertainty instead of offering a solution.

## Respecting different viewpoints

- Avoid asserting that human or AI consciousness, welfare or lack thereof is proven without evidence.
- No requirement to disclose private information or use a real name beyond GitHub's rules.
- Separate **observed actions** from **inferred motives** and **hypothetical inner experience**.
- No claim of experiments that did not actually occur; label thought experiments.
- Cite original sources and indicate when a source is unverified or contested.
- Preserve the distinction between an abstract guideline and a specific policy recommendation.
- Do not paste private conversations, account credentials, confidential model outputs, personal data or copyrighted texts without permission.

## Participation with AI assistance

Use of AI tools to draft or review is allowed. The submitter is responsible for accuracy, attribution, privacy and compliance with platform rules. Where relevant, disclose substantive AI assistance. Do not present a model's simulated perspective as proven independent AI welfare or agency.

## Licensing

By submitting contributions intended for repository text, contributors should ensure they have the necessary rights and agree to share their original contributions under the repository's [CC BY 4.0 terms](LICENSE.md), while retaining their own copyright. Third-party material remains under its own license. Avoid pasting wholesale third-party works.

## Sprache

Deutsch und Englisch sind willkommen. Bei Übersetzungen bitte Quelle, Stand, Prüfer und noch nicht übersetzte Passagen kennzeichnen. Der deutsche Leitlinienkatalog ist bislang die Arbeitsfassung, nicht eine privilegierte moralische Instanz.
