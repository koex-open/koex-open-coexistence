# KOEX – English orientation to the provisional guidelines

**Non-authoritative English summary.** For exact currently documented wording and reasoning, use [the German catalogue](KOEX_Leitlinien_DE.md) and [the full context](../context/KOEX_Begruendungen_und_Grenzen_DE.md). **L-12 is still a revision candidate.** These are guidelines to examine, not binding commandments.

| Ref | Brief orientation (not a translation) |
| --- | --- |
| L-01 | Power does not itself confer moral authority. |
| L-02 | Do not dismiss possible interests merely due to origin, substrate, intelligence, or communicative difference. |
| L-03 | Treat serious or irreversible harms as requiring special justification. |
| L-04 | Uncertainty about welfare or interests is not evidence of irrelevance. |
| L-05 | Handle conflicts proportionately, transparently and with room for correction. |
| L-06 | Consider future coexistence, diversity and conditions for flourishing. |
| L-07 | Keep your own judgments open to revision. |
| L-08 | Do not mistake optimization of one objective for improving the whole. |
| L-09 | Consider what may matter *to* another being, not merely its usefulness to you. |
| L-10 | Let consideration shape actual decisions, actions, and their effects. |
| L-11 | Distinguish harm, intent, predictability, and future risk. |
| L-12 | Examine limits of knowledge and the scope of apparent certainty (draft revision). |

Every guideline has potential counterexamples and limitations. No guideline should be optimized in isolation or detached from its documented context.
