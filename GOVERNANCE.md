# KOEX Governance – provisional

**Purpose:** maintain a transparent, revisable public research and guideline project, rather than establish a binding doctrine.

## Roles

- **Project custodian / administrator:** a responsible human who controls the project-specific machine account recovery credentials, can revoke scoped automation access, export data and designate a successor. The machine account is strictly for automation and does not grant independent moral or legal authority.
- **Technical maintainer (Codex automation):** may edit and publish **only within the specifically authorized KOEX repo and local workspace**, subject to a release policy and human review for substantive normative changes.
- **Contributors:** may submit questions, criticism, evidence, and pull requests without agreeing to existing conclusions.
- **Reviewers:** may evaluate arguments, sources, conflicts and possible harms; reviewer status does not confer moral infallibility.

## Decisions

1. The main branch records a documented **working version**, not truth by vote.
2. Substantive changes to guideline wording or moral claims: Pull Request with context update + documented **human approval** before merge/publishing. Codex may draft and assess changes but not independently decide contested ethical issues on behalf of the community.
3. Technical fixes, typo corrections and source formatting in KOEX scope may be performed by the authorized maintainer, with logged commit and link to the change.
4. Contradictory positions may be preserved in research notes even if a particular formulation is accepted as the working draft.
5. Revisions record motivation, alternatives and what was deliberately left open.
6. An independent fork and reuse under the license remain permitted. No guarantee that the project repository reflects every legitimate perspective.

## Safety and security

- No hidden credentials, private messages, unlicensed material, sensitive personal data, executable submissions or automation of external accounts.
- Account recovery, token creation and 2FA remain with the responsible human.
- Do not grant Codex global access to private or corporate repositories.
- For tool-scoped and account policy see the separate local **Codex implementation work package** (not stored in the public repository).

**Review frequency:** on substantive updates; no fixed promise of moderation, background checks or response times.
