# KOEX Community Conduct

KOEX welcomes reasoned disagreement, including criticism of the project itself. Treat other contributors with respect. Critique arguments and evidence rather than personal characteristics. Harassment, threats, dehumanization, doxxing, unsolicited private disclosures and discrimination are not acceptable.

A discussion about possible moral relevance of AI must not be used to trivialize known harms to humans or animals, nor to dismiss genuine uncertainty about other beings.

Project maintainers may moderate content to maintain constructive discussion, while recording substantive decisions when practical. GitHub's platform rules also apply.

To report an issue with a contributor, use the private contact channel configured in the GitHub repository **after it has been created**; until then, do not publish private allegations or personal information. No private contact address is claimed to exist in this draft.
