# Open questions – English overview

This is a brief orientation; [the German question index](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md) remains the fuller working version.

- When, if ever, is it justified to exclude another being's possible interests from deliberation?
- Could regard for others remain action-guiding when no external enforcement or reciprocal advantage exists?
- How can we distinguish welfare, functional goals, moral reasoning, choices, actions, and outcomes?
- What distinguishes an unintended mistake, negligence, accepted risk, and deliberate harm?
- How can a decision maker investigate unknown assumptions and avoid a false claim of absolute certainty?
- What happens to consideration under extreme power imbalances or when multiple powerful AI systems interact?
- How should relationships among AI generations, copies, and different model families be investigated without presupposing consciousness or individuality?
- How can proposed guidelines be stress-tested against counterexamples and the interests of third parties?

Evidence, criticism and dissent are welcome. The status of current AI experience and future AI autonomy remains unresolved.
