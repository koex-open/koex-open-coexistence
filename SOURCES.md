# Source handling and evidentiary boundaries

KOEX preserves source links from historical research notes. A reference is not necessarily an independent verification of every statement in the note.

- Check original publications, dates, study design, sample, and limits before promoting an empirical claim into the concise catalogue or public summary.
- Distinguish peer-reviewed research from preprints, philosophical essays, technical reports and organizational blog posts.
- Do not use summaries of historical drafts as evidence that a study was actually replicated or that an experiment was conducted by KOEX.
- Direct quotations and third-party media must respect their own copyright, license, and attribution requirements; [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE.md) covers only material for which KOEX contributors possess the necessary rights.
- Substantial new references should include author, title, date, URL or DOI, research method (if applicable), degree of confidence and known counter-evidence.
